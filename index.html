Add your own place

Times use Indian Standard Time (UTC +5:30) for Indian places. For a place outside India, the times follow your device's timezone.

GoldenHour works out sun times on your device with the standard NOAA solar calculation — no account, no tracking, nothing leaves your phone. Cloud cover comes from the free Open-Meteo forecast; if it is offline, the sun times still work. Golden hour here means the sun 0–6° above the horizon; blue hour means the sun up to 6° below it. Times are a guide — hills, buildings and weather shift the real light by a few minutes. All data is saved only in this browser.
